# SCR-002: Scheduled weekly "business review" digest from the assistant
Filed by: product-manager  Date: 2026-09-26  Type: add
Scope section affected: scope.md#mvp-in item 13 ("AI business assistant (read-only tools)")

## Request
Send each shop a weekly digest (email or in-app notification) generated by the assistant, proactively, on a schedule — the same "business review" content wave 17's analyst mode already produces on request ("Give me a weekly business review" is one of the four starter questions in T-17-4), but pushed automatically instead of asked for. Raised in `specs/assistant-business-analyst.md` ("Out of scope") during wave 17 planning.

## Why (evidence)
- `research/03-pain-points.md`: "unknown profit" is a top-ranked pain; a recurring digest could reduce the effort of checking it. No pilot or ticket has asked for a scheduled digest specifically — evidence here is inferred from the pain ranking, not a direct ask. 0 shops confirmed.
- Wave 17 already builds the underlying capability (analyst mode's ranked-recommendation format) for the on-demand case; this request is only about adding a *push* surface on top of it.

## Who it helps
Mid shops most (owner/office does not always remember to open the assistant); small shops less (fewer channels/designs to review); large shops probably need this filtered per department, which is more work than this SCR covers.

## Cost and risk
- **New surface, not a tool:** scope item 13 says "AI business assistant (read-only tools)" — a tool the owner calls. A scheduled digest is a different shape: an outbound, unattended job that generates content and sends it (email) or surfaces it (notification) without a user turn. That needs its own spec (delivery channel, opt-out, frequency, quiet hours), not just a tool.
- **Recurring AI spend:** one assistant-analyst-mode run per shop per week, unattended, for every shop on the platform — this is a new per-tenant recurring AI cost line that doesn't exist today (today's AI spend is triggered by a user action). Needs sizing against `PLAN_CATALOG` AI credit allowances before it ships (`unit-economics-model`).
- **Job/ops risk:** a new scheduled job (BullMQ) touching every tenant weekly; if the underlying analyst-mode logic changes, every shop's digest changes without a user in the loop to notice something's wrong — needs its own evals and a "hold" rule if AI output looks degraded.
- **Outbound content risk:** an automatically emailed "recommendation" that turns out wrong (e.g., mis-attributed ad ROAS) reaches the shop with no chance for a human (owner or customer-success) to check it first, unlike the on-request case where the shop reads it in context.
- **Effort:** a full card at minimum (schedule, email/notification delivery, opt-out, an eval gate before send) — not free, and not currently in any wave.

## If we don't
Shops still get the same content by asking the assistant (the "Give me a weekly business review" starter question ships in wave 17); they just have to remember to ask.

## Decision
**Accepted (owner approved, 2026-09-27, OI-7), in a changed shape.** The owner: "that's actually a good idea. Research deeply how to implement it... then build it." Guardrails: its own spec, opt-in/opt-out, an eval gate before any unattended send, a spending cap per tenant.
- Research: `research/15-weekly-digest.md`. It replaces this request's "re-run the analyst assistant on a timer" with a hybrid: numbers and ranked actions computed in code, en/es templates, and an optional AI summary that only phrases placeholders. The AI summary runs in shadow mode (never sent) until the real-model eval (OI-8) passes.
- Scope: `product/scope.md#weekly-digest` (item 17). Spec: `specs/weekly-digest.md`. Decision record: `decisions/0014-market-signals-and-digest-scope.md`.
- Before the first real email to a pilot: postal address (OI-12), production email provider and sending subdomain (OI-13), counsel's view on CAN-SPAM classification (OI-14). In-app delivery and Mailpit locally need none of these.
