## Acceptance criteria (spec AC numbers in brackets)
1. **Tables:** tenant tables (`company_id` + RLS + tenant-isolation test) for design niches, price snapshots (90-day raw, monthly roll-up), signals `(company_id, subject_type, subject_id, signal, source)`, recommendations (rule, target, action params, confidence, band, signals snapshot + sources, 28-day baseline, shown_in, shown_at, vote, adoption, outcome). `market_series_cache` is global per ADR 0015 (no tenant column; taxonomy queries only), written only by the nightly job under `withSystem`, idempotent on `(source, query, granularity, period)`. [AC23]
2. **Jobs (queue, never in the request path):** `market.refreshDemand` (nightly 03:00 UTC, global), `market.refreshPricing` (daily per shop per active Amazon/Walmart connection; mocks now), `market.computeSignals` (per shop; stable job id per shop and date, so one run per shop per day; also queued when a design is created or its niche is corrected), `market.trackRecommendations` (daily per shop). A scheduler finds shops with no signals for today, so a freshly seeded stack has signals within one scheduler tick (state the tick; ≤ 1 h). Module `jobs.ts` self-registers its schedulers at import (like `today/jobs.ts`); subscribe to the existing `design.updated` event for new designs (no new outbox event). Running any job twice gives one effect. [AC1]
2b. **S-34 (security, T-18-1 co-review):** `market.refreshDemand` iterates the entire fixed taxonomy (`NICHES[*].queries × sources`) every run, never a subset derived from which niches tenants use, so the cache's contents reveal nothing about any shop. A test asserts the fetched query set equals the taxonomy's.
2a. **Mock visibility rule** (`wave.md` hard fences; spec AC29): `mockSourcesAllowed(companyId)` = `!env.isProd || env.allowMocks || await isSampleWorkspace(companyId)`. When false, mock demand rows are skipped in `computeSignals` and reads, `refreshPricing` doesn't store mock comparables, R2/R4 don't fire, and `getPricePosition` returns `available: false, reason: no_compliant_source`. Unit test both branches (production-like env with a real shop vs a sample workspace). Recommendations built on mock data carry the sample flag so the text says "Sample data" [AC30].
3. **Mapper:** stem match against the taxonomy (≤ 2 niches, most matched stems), else `classifyDesignNiche` accepted at confidence ≥ 0.7 (its confidence multiplies into signals), else `unclassified`. A shop correction always wins and survives re-runs; no AI credits → `unclassified` and own-data signals still compute. Every niche label and canonical query goes through `screenMarketTerms` before it is queried or shown; drops are counted in logs. [AC1, AC12, AC21, AC25]
4. **Own history:** weekly units, revenue, net, average landed price per design × channel, 156 weeks, shop time zone ISO weeks. Excludes cancelled items (including cancelled after `on_sheet`), sample-workspace and test orders, and reprints. Refunded items count as units. Weeks the design's blank was out of stock are excluded from the trend fit. [AC17, AC18]
5. **Signal engine (pure functions, unit-tested on fixed series, not on the same SQL):** trend (OLS on ln(y+1), 26 or 13 weeks, deseasonalized when SI exists; rising/falling/flat/insufficient per spec thresholds), yoy, seasonality index (source priority own → outside → Census prior) with peak/off months and act-by date from the shop's median paid→shipped lead time + 3 weeks, price position (percentile, Q1/median/Q3, min n 8, comparable filter incl. personalization flag and IQR outliers), competition density terciles (Amazon only), margin at price (5¢ grid, break-even, floor at 15% margin, rounded to the shop's ending), price-response estimate only with ≥ 2 price points of ≥ 30 units. [AC3, AC5, AC7, AC19]
6. **Confidence:** `s·f·r·a` with the spec's targets, half-lives and reliabilities; bands high ≥ 0.70, medium 0.40–0.69, low < 0.40; disagreement between sources sets a = 0.4 and a flag, never averaged away; signals older than 2× their source TTL read as `stale: true` with the freshness factor applied. [AC8, AC10]
7. **Rules R1–R5** exactly as the spec table, with fixed action params (no free text), R1 naming the blank and reorder link when the blank is below its reorder point. A low-band signal never produces a recommendation (unit test per rule). [AC3, AC4, AC9, AC18]
8. **`simulatePrice`** always available from own data (trailing 90 days of profit data: fees at price p, unit cost, shipping charged, ads per unit, refund rate); `incomplete` when costs are missing; numbers equal a hand calculation from fixed fixtures. [AC4, AC5]
9. **`getPricePosition`** returns `available: false` with a reason for Etsy, TikTok and Shopify, for a channel not connected, and below n = 8. [AC6]
10. **Outage:** when a provider throws (T-18-2's `MARKET_MOCK_FAIL`), the refresh records the failure, keeps the last good cache rows, refreshes other sources, and reads show the older `asOf` with lower confidence; nothing throws to a user. [AC20]
11. **Feedback:** adoption rules (price ≥ 3% in the suggested direction within 14 days; new active listing on the suggested channel within 21; new design in the niche within 30); an explicit vote always wins and voting twice stores one vote; 28 days after adoption an outcome label by difference-in-differences against the shop's other active designs of the same garment class. [AC26, AC27]
12. **Router and permissions:** procedures per T-18-1 under `withTenant` (`recommendations.list` cursor-paginated, `ids` filter for reload of a stored message's votes; each item has `vote` and `votedAt`); designer can set a niche and gets `FORBIDDEN` on recommendations; presser `FORBIDDEN` on all; another shop's design id → `NOT_FOUND`. [AC24]
13. **Read-only by construction:** no code path in `src/modules/market/**` writes to catalog, listings, channels, finance, inventory or purchasing tables; a test lists the module's writes (only market tables). [AC16]
14. **Digest read service:** `listDigestMarketItems` returns only R1–R5 items with band ≥ medium for the shop's own designs/niches, trademark-screened, not stale, with source, asOf, band and mock (wave 19 consumes it). Unit-tested.
15. **Scale (budget check, not the full AC28 run):** time `computeSignals` for a synthetic shop of 5,000 designs and 3 years of weekly history built in your test DB; report the time. Target within 15 minutes; QA runs the full AC28 profile in its scale run.
