- **Taxonomy** `niches.ts`: the 69 niches, converted mechanically from the JSON in `product/market-niches.md`, plus `nicheLabel`, `nicheByKey` and `CANONICAL_QUERIES`.
- **Pure engine**, unit-tested on fixed series in `engine.test.ts`:
  - `signals.ts`: complete ISO weeks in the shop's time zone; OLS trend on ln(y/SI+1) over 26 or 13 weeks; yoy; seasonality index, peaks and off months; act-by; comparable filter ($5–$80, personalization flag, IQR); percentile and quartiles; density terciles; net(p), margin, break-even and 15% floor on the 5¢ grid with .99/.00 endings; candidate prices; arc elasticity.
  - `confidence.ts`: s·f·r·a, bands, stale at 2× TTL.
  - `mapper.ts`: stem match after accent stripping, then the model at ≥ 0.7, else unclassified.
  - `rules.ts`: R1–R5.
  - `config.ts` holds every threshold (`MARKET_CONFIG`) and the one predicate `mockSourcesAllowed(companyId)`.
- **Compute** (`compute.ts`, `history.ts`, `read.ts`):
  - Own history comes from read-only aggregates. It excludes cancelled items and orders, including items cancelled after `on_sheet`, and reprints. Refunded items still count.
  - Out-of-stock weeks come from the inventory ledger walked back from today's on-hand; those weeks drop out of the fit.
  - Niche outside series are the mean of the niche's screened queries, aggregated in SQL.
  - Margin uses `getProfit` (trailing 90 days) plus the fee model (`orderFees`, referral schedules) read without inserting `cost_settings`.
  - Price position comes from the latest snapshot. Everything is persisted and rules R1–R5 run.
  - Recommendations are not re-created for the same rule and target within 28 days.
- **Jobs** (`jobs.ts`, all on the `reports` queue; the modules' jobs file imports it):
  - `market.refreshDemand`: global, 03:00 UTC scheduler, `withSystem` with an ADR 0015 comment. It refuses non-taxonomy queries, skips a source still inside its TTL, and when a provider fails it keeps the last good rows and records the failure. Retention is 5 years.
  - `market.refreshPricing`: per shop. Amazon and Walmart only; the provider call runs outside any transaction; mocks are skipped where not allowed; roll-up after 90 days.
  - `market.computeSignals`: stable id per shop and date. It also runs design-scoped on `design.updated` and after a niche correction.
  - `market.trackRecommendations`: daily per shop.
  - `market.sweep`: hourly. It finds shops with no signals for their local today and queues demand → pricing → signals → track, so the tick is ≤ 1 hour.
  - A per-shop job for a company that no longer exists throws `UnrecoverableError`.
- **Feedback** (`feedback.ts`):
  - Adoption: R2/R3 when the price moves ≥ 3% up within 14 days; R1 when a new active listing appears on a suggested channel within 21 days; R4 when a new design lands in the niche within 30 days.
  - The vote always wins: "done" counts as adopted at vote time, and "not useful" is never auto-adopted.
  - Outcome: after 28 days, a difference-in-differences against same-garment-class controls from the baseline; "improved" needs ≥ 10 units after. Guarded by state, so re-runs change nothing.
  - Retention: 400 days.
- **Service** (`service.ts`, the agreed interfaces, all `(tx, ctx, input)`):

  - Digest: `listDigestMarketItems({asOf})` returns R1–R5 at band ≥ medium, not stale, from the last 7 days, trademark-screened.
  - Niches: `nicheTaxonomy`, `getDesignNiches`, `setDesignNiches`.

## Decisions
- `n` on a trend reading = weeks with sales in the history (spec's "points"); zero weeks count only for the `mostly_zero` test.
- Weeks are the last *complete* ISO weeks in the shop time zone; the week in progress is left out.
- Own trends are deseasonalized only by an own seasonality index. An outside or Census index describes someone else's demand, and dividing own units by it would invent trends.
- The trademark screen runs per shop in `computeSignals` on every niche label and query. The global fetch sends only canonical taxonomy queries, and `screenMarketTerms` needs a `companyId`. A dropped label or query is never mapped, read or shown.
- Census freshness is measured from `fetchedAt`, because the series lags by design; every other source from `asOf`.
- Agreement 0.7 ("single source") applies only to directional trend readings; margin, price and seasonality use a = 1.
- Own data in a sample workspace carries `mock: true`, so it is shown as sample data. The schema has no test-order flag, so none are excluded.
- `products.prices[].price` is cents (contracts `Cents`); the seed confirmed it.
- Recommendations are created by `computeSignals` (QA expects them after the jobs); "shown" is recorded separately by `recordRecommendationsShown`.
- Other modules' tables are read, never written: orders, items, profit_lines, listings, products, connections, blanks, stock, movements, cost_settings (the `today` / assistant-tools pattern). I don't call `lowStockItems` and `ensureCostSettings` because both insert rows.
