## Acceptance criteria
1. **Chips:** tool chips for the 4 market tools ("Market trend", "Seasonality", "Price position", "Price simulation" / "Tendencia del mercado", "Temporada", "Posición de precio", "Simulación de precio").
2. **Starters:** the 3 market starter questions from the spec copy table, in en and es, alongside the wave 17 starters (keep the list readable at 390 px).
3. **Sample data badge:** any answer whose tool results carry `mock: true` shows the "Sample data" / "Datos de muestra" badge near the answer (not hidden in a tooltip).
4. **Votes** (spec AC33): vote cards render from `tool_result.recommendations` (not parsed from text): band label and "Done" / "Not useful" buttons (accessible names include the action) calling `market.recommendations.vote`; a second tap shows the stored state, not a second vote; after reloading the conversation (`AssistantMessage.recommendations` + `market.recommendations.list({ids})`) the voted state shows. Stale signals show the spec's stale copy.
5. **Niche chip** (spec AC32): the design page shows 0, 1 or 2 niche chips; "Change" opens one searchable picker (existing `Command`/cmdk component, en/es labels) that edits both (max 2) and calls `market.niches.set`, with a confirmation; 0 niches shows "No niche yet. Pick one to get market signals."; users without `market.niches.manage` don't see "Change".
6. **Permissions in the UI:** a designer never sees recommendation votes or price data; the page doesn't error for them.
7. **Copy and layout:** every string en + es from the spec copy table (as corrected by the spec reviews), 390 px and 1440 px, light and dark, no raw keys, no English in Spanish mode.
