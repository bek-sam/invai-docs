- `.claude/agent-memory/<role>/MEMORY.md` seeds

## Acceptance criteria
1. `verify-and-report` and `independent-review` end with a "Save what you learned" step: 0–3 short entries in your agent memory (a mistake you made and how you fixed it, or a non-obvious fact about your area), each with the date and card. No PII, secrets or customer data. Update or remove entries that turned out wrong.
2. The agent brief has a short "Memory" section saying the same.
3. Each role that builds or reviews code has a seeded `MEMORY.md`: 5–10 lessons from `team/lessons.md` that apply to that role, one line each, with the lesson date. Under 60 lines per file.
4. `sync.sh backup` also copies `.claude/agent-memory/` to `invai-docs/team/agent-memory/`, and `restore` copies it back.
