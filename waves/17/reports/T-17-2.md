## Built
- Four read-only tools in `assistantTools(ctx)`, each in its own `withTenant` transaction, every list capped at 20 rows, money in cents and ratios 0..1 in `data`, formatted text in `summary`/`answer` (file: invai-backend/src/modules/ai/assistant-tools.ts):
  - `compare_periods` (`from`, `to`, optional `previousFrom`/`previousTo`, `channel`): orders, units, revenue, net, margin, ads cost and average order value per period; absolute and % change (`pct: null` when the previous value is 0); per-channel revenue and net contributions that add up to the total change. Default previous period = same length right before (`previousPeriod`). Built on `getProfit` (dimension channel), so numbers match the profit page, refunds included.
  - `get_ad_performance` (range, `channel`, `groupBy: channel | campaign`): spend from `ad_spend`, channel revenue and orders, ROAS = channel revenue ÷ spend (null at 0 spend), TACoS = spend ÷ total shop revenue, ad cost per order, net before and after ads, flags `spend_with_negative_net` and `spend_up_revenue_down` (vs the previous same-length period), `attribution: "channel"`. Campaign grouping returns campaign, channel, spend and share of spend only.
  - `get_design_insights` (range, `limit` ≤ 20): rising and falling (≥ 3 units, vs previous same-length period), low margin (< 15%, ≥ 3 units), top net, cross-listing gaps (≥ 3 units on one channel, no active listing on another channel with an active connection; listings matched directly or through `listing_variants`). Channels whose connections are all pending/disconnected are left out and reported in `inactiveChannels`, with `incomplete: true` and `incompleteReasons: ["channel_not_connected:<ch>"]` (plus `missing_cost_data` when profit is incomplete).
  - `get_fulfillment_health` (range, `channel`): on-time rate per channel (orders shipped in the period, `shippedAt <= shipBy`, cancelled excluded), late count, median hours placed → shipped (per channel and overall, `percentile_cont`), open orders overdue now (same open statuses as `get_orders_summary`), reprints by reason with rate per item placed in the period and an estimated cost (item's transfer cost split over its prints, plus the blank when `blankConsumed`), refunds per channel with voided ones excluded.
- Mock routing (file: invai-backend/src/ai/providers/mock.ts `planAssistantCalls`): ads/ad spend/ROAS/TACoS/campaign/anuncio → `get_ad_performance` (campaign → `groupBy: "campaign"`); compare/vs/versus/why/por qué → `compare_periods`; rising/falling/trending/push or drop/cross-list/low margin/"designs" (not "best selling designs")/diseños → `get_design_insights`; on time/late/reprint/refund/fulfil/a tiempo/reimpresión/reembolso → `get_fulfillment_health`; "business review"/"what should I do" → all four. "this week vs last week" compares week-to-date with the same days of last week. Existing routes kept (existing `ai.test.ts` planner test unchanged and green).

## Decisions
- TACoS divides by total shop revenue (all channels), so per-channel TACoS values add up to the shop's TACoS; per channel it would otherwise be exactly 1/ROAS. Stated in the tool description.
- Ad spend days are shop-local days, half-open (`day >= localDay(from)`, `day < localDay(to)`), the way the profit allocation reads `ad_spend`; back-to-back periods never count a day twice.
- "Net after ads" = channel net before allocated ads minus the actual `ad_spend` in the period (spend on days with no orders is not lost).
- On-time rate counts orders **shipped** in the period (what Etsy/Amazon measure); overdue-now uses the same open statuses as `get_orders_summary` so the two tools agree.
- Active connection = `connected`, `csv_only` or `error` (the seed's Etsy/Amazon/TikTok are `csv_only` and have listings); the `csv` channel is never a cross-listing target.
- Reprint cost is an estimate (labelled "about" in the answer): the item's transfer cost split over its prints, plus the blank when it was consumed.
- Eval seeding lives in `evals/assistant/seed.ts` (a second tenant) so the zero-state cases keep the empty tenant.
