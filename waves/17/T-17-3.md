## Acceptance criteria
1. **Tool memory:** earlier assistant turns in `history` carry one compact line naming the tools used and their `summary` (from `assistant_messages.toolCalls`). Scrubbed like other history, at most 600 chars per turn. The 20-message limit stays.
2. **Shop context block:** the shop's time zone, "today" in that zone, connected channels and currency USD are sent as a second system block **after** the cached prefix. A test proves the cached system text is byte-identical across two shops.
3. **Language:** the prompt tells the model to reply in the user's language (English or Spanish). An eval case asks in Spanish.
4. **Analyst mode:** the prompt adds the spec's recommendation structure (finding, evidence, action, expected impact as a labelled estimate, at most 3), tells the model to call independent tools in the same turn, and adds the honesty rules (incomplete data, channel-level ad attribution, no outside market facts, no promised results).
5. `ASSISTANT_PROMPT.version` becomes 4. `max_iterations` becomes 10.
6. The mock provider still gives deterministic answers.
7. Evals: assistant evals run in mock mode with the baseline updated. Add cases for a follow-up question and a "weekly business review". Every earlier case still passes.
8. Remove the `get_production_status` "not in the contract" workaround in `service.ts` once T-17-1 has landed.
