## Decisions
- Placement anchor = top-centre of the print area (how a presser lines a transfer up), not its middle.
- `fill_ratio` = bounding box share on the limiting side (Amazon's 85% reading); compliance-officer to confirm.
- `background_pure_white` grows the product to whole 8×8 JPEG blocks (4:4:4), where ringing lives; everything else must be exactly 255.
- Company prefix = first key segment, must be a UUID; violation is 400 (card), the only non-422 error.
- Output format follows `out_key` extension (.jpg/.jpeg → JPEG, .png → PNG, else 422).
- Folded view: real-size design, the part below the fold is hidden (not shrunk).
