## Decisions
- Kept the prior session's choice of a hand-rolled Markdown parser over a library (architect-approved,
  T-21-5 review); did not add a dependency.
- `/help` and `/legal` are top-level routes outside `/_app` (not gated by the auth guard in
  `_app.tsx`), since a signed-out visitor must be able to read Terms before creating an account, and
  the card calls them "Public routes". The app-shell Help entry still links there for signed-in
  users; they lose the authenticated app chrome on that page, same trade-off `login.tsx`/`signup.tsx`
  already make.
- Built a small `PublicContentLayout` in `src/content/` (an owned path) rather than reusing/extending
  `AppFrame` or `AuthLayout`, to avoid touching files outside this card's owned globs.
- Legal doc titles in the four-way nav come from each document's own parsed `doc.title`, not new i18n
  keys, since docs-writer/compliance-officer already own that text and duplicating it in `en.ts`/`es.ts`
  would be a second place to keep in sync.
- Dropped the body's leading `# Title` heading block when rendering both help articles and legal docs,
  since the frontmatter `title` (or first heading, for legal drafts with no frontmatter) is the same
  text and would otherwise render twice.
