## Decisions
- **"Receiving" targets the floor Receiving station**, not the web Purchase Orders page alone. The
  floor app (`invai-floor/src/i18n/en.ts`) has a dedicated **Receiving** station with three tabs
  (Purchase orders, Vendor transfers, Stock count) that a `receiver` role uses on a tablet — that's
  richer and matches the seeded `receiver@desertbloom.test` login and floor PINs, so I wrote the
  article for that flow and cross-linked it from the web-side gang-sheets article ("Mark received" on a
  vendor sheet) instead of duplicating it. Card-local, no ADR.
- **13 articles, not exactly 12**: the card's list names 13 distinct topics ("gang sheets *and vendors*"
  and "AI listing drafts *and* the trademark check" read as combined topics, but "weekly digest",
  "team and roles" and "plans and billing" are each their own). I kept them as 13 separate slugs rather
  than merging any, since each has its own screen and audience; still satisfies "at least 12."
- **Seed-time correction (15–20 min → ~25–100 s)**: T-20-5's report measured the seed at 25–100 seconds
  multiple times back-to-back on the same UTC day; the runbook's "15–20 minutes" predates that and was
  never updated. Fixed it and said so in the text (code/evidence wins over the old doc), rather than
  silently changing the number.
- **Kept the placeholder tokens untranslated in Spanish** (`{{name}}`, `{{blank}}`, `{{channel}}`,
  `{{poNo}}`, `{{day}}`, not `{{nombre}}`/`{{prenda}}`/etc.) — caught this in my own first draft and
  fixed it: the real `es.ts`/floor `es.ts` catalogs never translate the interpolation key, only the
  surrounding text (`write-plain-language-copy`'s rule). Verified with a `grep -o '{{[^}]*}}'` sweep on
  both languages before committing.
