# T-13-4: E2E coverage and CI (B-22, B-97)

## CI time-budget check (r1, product-manager + architect review — full evidence in `wave.md`)
**Not realistic as scoped without changes.** Both web and floor `playwright.config.ts` are
`workers: 1, fullyParallel: false`; this card adds ~14 web flows, 4 floor flows, 5 role specs, 2 Spanish
smoke passes and axe scans on top of a baseline that's already ~1 min (web) serially. AC6 also stands up
E2E in backend/web/floor CI **from zero** (none run E2E today), so each of the three independent GitHub
jobs pays its own checkout/install/service-boot cost before a single test runs. Recommendation:
(1) treat "under 15 minutes" as **per workflow**, not summed across the three jobs; (2) turn on
`fullyParallel: true` / `workers: 2-4` for the state-independent additions (property tests, axe scans);
keep the golden-path/API spec serial; (3) explicitly budget imaging + worker startup in the CI job, since
neither is there today.
Owns (batch 1, parallel-safe): `invai-web/e2e/**` + `playwright.config.ts` + its CI workflow;
`invai-floor/e2e/**` + `playwright.config.ts` + its CI workflow; the new e2e job in
`invai-backend/.github/workflows/ci.yml`; the Shopify OAuth-state flaky test.

## Acceptance criteria
1. **Roles:** an E2E spec per role (office, designer, presser, packer, receiver): hidden nav, 403s and redirects.
2. **Spanish:** web and floor golden-path smoke in Spanish, with no English leaks (a text check).
3. **Uncovered flows:** web hold, release and cancel; PO receive; stock adjust; design upload; team invite and accept; pairing and PINs; plan change; vendor invite; void; batch buy; tracking export; refund record. Floor pick, QC fail and reprint, idle lock.
4. **Property tests:** money math, sizes and nesting (fast-check).
5. **Axe in E2E:** fails on serious or critical issues.
6. **CI:** E2E runs in the backend, web and floor GitHub workflows (services: postgres, valkey, minio; seed; the suites), and stays under 15 minutes.
7. **Flaky tests:** the Shopify OAuth-state timing tests become load-tolerant.
