# T-13-5: Realistic seed efficiency (B-111) and seed locale

## Root cause (r1, product-manager + architect review — confirmed, full evidence in `wave.md`)
`invai-backend/src/db/seed/builder.ts:1038-1054`'s greedy row-packing (introduced by the T-5-3 reusable
seed-builder refactor, commit `72c1139`) replaced the pre-refactor **fixed-2-per-row** layout (commit
`a061c46`, the one that got 86–91%) with a width-overflow shelf pack over `chunk` sorted only by
`placedAt` — never by width. A wide item landing mid-row after narrower ones forces an early wrap and
wastes the rest of that row. Two later, correct overhead fixes (`8b728b1`: `LABEL_HEIGHT_IN` 0.35→0.42in;
`5ba3c8f`: new 0.45in `HEADER_HEIGHT_IN` band) compound it further but should **not** be undone — they're
production-accuracy fixes.
**Fix:** sort each `PER_SHEET` chunk by `widthIn` descending (first-fit-decreasing) before the row-fill
loop. Verify against 85–92% on a full 300-order seed, not a spot check.
Owns exclusively (batch 1, parallel-safe): `invai-backend/src/db/seed/builder.ts`, `src/db/seed/data.ts`.

## Acceptance criteria
1. **Film efficiency:** the seed produces film efficiency of 85–92% on full sheets again (the realistic art-size mix per research 01). Find what the T-5-3 builder refactor changed.
2. **Locale:** Spanish floor staff in the seed have `locale: "es"`.
3. **Demo workspaces:** the demo builder (T-5-3) gets the same realistic mix.
4. **Unchanged elsewhere:** the golden-path numbers still pass, and the seed runs in under 60 s.
