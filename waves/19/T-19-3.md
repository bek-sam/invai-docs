## Acceptance criteria (spec AC numbers in brackets)
1. **Tables** (company_id + RLS + isolation test): digests (one per shop per week key, unique), insights, feedback (idempotent, latest wins), clicks, deliveries (unique per digest, person, channel; skip reason), shop settings. [AC2, AC28]
2. **Schedule:** hourly sweep; slot from shop time zone and settings (default Monday 07:00; hour 6–10), DST computed in the database; sample workspaces (`isSampleWorkspace`) and soft-deleted shops skipped; catch-up until 20:00 local, after that in-app only with delivery `skipped` reason `quiet_hours`; never email 20:00–07:00 local. Building twice → one digest; delivering twice → one email per person. [AC1–AC5]
3. **Snapshot** for last ISO week (shop time), previous week and trailing 4 (up to 8) weeks, using `analyst-queries.ts` and finance's profit functions; glance net equals the profit page's net for that week (parity test against the finance service, not the same SQL). Cancelled-after-`on_sheet` adds nothing; reprints show as reprints. [AC1, AC10]
4. **Detectors D1–D8** with thresholds in `config.ts` and minimum-volume guards; D1 one row per disconnected channel, pinned first, glance says "may be partial"; D6 counts equal a hand SQL count; D7 names the blank and links to reorder; small-shop case gives "A steady week". [AC6–AC8, AC11]
5. **Ranking:** score = impact × confidence × severity; ≤ 3 actions, 1 win, glance block; repeat suppression (voted down last week → demoted; shown 3 weeks with no action → out of top 3); skip rule and `skipped_quiet`, "paused" line after 2 quiet weeks. [AC9, AC12]
6. **Market watch:** from `listDigestMarketItems`: ≤ 2 items, R1–R5, band ≥ medium, own designs/niches, trademark-screened, mock visibility rule; R1 with a cross-listing gap or low blank and R3 may take a top-3 slot, R2/R4 never; each shown item recorded via `recordRecommendationsShown(..., "digest", refId=digestId)`; missing/stale/throwing market read → block absent, digest still `ready`. [AC14–AC17, AC31]
7. **Templates** en/es from the spec copy table; subject written by code; Spanish recipients get Spanish (money stays USD); no raw keys. [AC13]
8. **AI summary:** call `generateDigestNarrative` per spec cost rules (skip on quiet weeks or when nobody will read it); store status and failed rules; in shadow mode the stored text is never returned by any procedure or rendered (test asserts rendered output equals the template). [AC18, AC21]
9. **Delivery:** `digest.ready` event on store; email only to opted-in members with `finance.read` via `sendUserEmail` (unsubscribe headers, deterministic Message-ID from digest + person); each skip recorded with its reason; office user who opts in gets next week's email. [AC23, AC26]
10. **Router/permissions** per T-19-1; presser/designer `FORBIDDEN`; office sees no plan usage; office refused on settings; another shop's week key → `NOT_FOUND`; preview rate-limited to the caller. [AC27, AC30]
11. **Clicks:** register a `click` link handler (T-19-4) that records the click and redirects to the insight's in-app href (same-origin only).
12. **Scale (budget check):** time one build for a synthetic 1,000 orders/day shop (target < 60 s) and a sweep over 1,000 small shops in your test DB; QA runs the full AC29 profile separately.
