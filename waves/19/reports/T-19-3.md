## Built
- **Tables** (`src/db/schema/digest.ts`, `drizzle/0029_digest.sql`): `digest_settings`, `digests` (unique company+week key), `digest_insights`, `digest_feedback` (unique insight+person, latest wins), `digest_clicks` (unique insight+person, first wins), `digest_views` (first view per person → `viewedAt`), `digest_deliveries` (unique digest+person+channel, status + skip reason). All have `company_id`, the tenant policy and RLS. Children use composite `(company_id, digest_id|insight_id)` FKs (S-26). Retention: nightly `digest.purge` deletes digests older than 104 weeks (cascade).
- **Schedule** (`jobs.ts`): `digest.sweep` hourly (scheduler `5 * * * *`); one SQL finds shops whose local time (`AT TIME ZONE`, DST from Postgres) is at/after their slot (default Mon 07:00, hour 6–10) with no finished digest for last week; skips vendor orgs, soft-deleted, disabled and sample workspaces (`realCompanySql()`). The sweep builds due shops in turn (one failure never stops the others; the next hour retries). `digest.build {companyId, weekKey}` is the single-shop/forced path.
- **Build** (`build.ts`): row created once, built under `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`; `ready`/`skipped_quiet` rows are never rebuilt. Stores insights + content, records Market watch items as shown, emits `digest.ready` (outbox) and publishes realtime after commit. Then, outside any transaction, the shadow AI summary (`generateDigestNarrative`), skipped on quiet weeks or when nobody will read it; only `narrative_status`/`narrative` are updated and neither is ever selected by a procedure.
- **Snapshot** (`snapshot.ts`): `comparePeriods`, `adPerformance`, `designInsights`, `fulfillmentHealth` (T-19-2) and `getProfit` (trailing 8 weeks by day for cost lines and trailing nets), `lowStockItems` (inventory).
- **Detectors** (`detectors.ts`, thresholds in `config.ts`): D1–D8 with minimum-volume guards; D1 one row per unhealthy connection; D6 overdue count at the build instant; D7 links low blanks to top designs through their recent sales (unlinked fallback at confidence 0.5). New guard: when > 20% of the week's orders have no final fees, the money-based detectors (D2, D3, D4, D5 low-margin, D8 best-net) stay silent (found on the dev copy: 75 orders without profit lines made every ad channel look unprofitable).
- **Ranking** (`rank.ts`): score = impact × confidence × severity; D1 pinned; ≤ 3 actions, 1 win, ≤ 2 Market watch; one promotable market item (R1 with gap/low blank, R3) may take an action slot; voted down last week → × 0.5; shown 3 weeks running without a click → out of the top 3.
- **Market watch** (`market-watch.ts`): from `listDigestMarketItems` inside a savepoint (a throw leaves the block out; the digest is still `ready`); drops mock items in production; `recordRecommendationsShown(..., "digest", refId = digestId)`.
- **Templates** (`render.ts`): en/es table (spec keys + a few new keys marked `new:`), subject written by code, HTML escaped, money USD in both languages (`es-US`). Email footer comes from T-19-4's `emailFooter`.
- **Delivery** (`deliver.ts`): on `digest.ready` → `digest.deliver`; members with `finance.read` (by permission); a delivery row is recorded before the send and settled after; send idempotency is T-19-4's `email_sends` (dedupe `digest:${digestId}:${userId}`, Message-ID `<digest.${digestId}.${userId}@domain>`). Skips recorded: `opted_out`, `quiet_hours`, and `sendUserEmail`'s reasons. Built before 07:00 on the send day → delivery retries at the next hour; after 20:00 or a later day → `quiet_hours`.
- **Router** (`router.ts`): all 9 procedures implemented, `stubRouter` removed. Preview: Redis key `digest:preview:${companyId}:${userId}` 60 s → `RATE_LIMITED` with `Retry-After`; no digest → `NO_DIGEST`.
- **Click links:** `registerLinkHandler("click")` (ref `digestId:insightId`) records the click and returns the stored in-app href.

## Decisions
- The sweep builds due shops itself instead of fanning out one job per shop: measured 33 s for 1,000 shops, and it keeps the sweep test-observable; `digest.build` stays for single/forced builds.
- `withSystem` only in `dueShops` (cross-tenant ids) and `digest.purge` (retention by age), reasons written in code.
- Read-only reads of other modules' tables where no service answers at a controlled instant: `orders` (overdue at the build instant; the analyst query uses DB `now()`, which tests can't freeze; count of orders without profit lines), `channel_connections` (D1: `error`, disconnected this week, or `last_error_at` in the week), `profit_lines` (which blanks top designs used), `users`/`members` (recipients by permission, locale, verified, PIN-only). Architect to confirm or offer service functions.
- An on-time slip with nothing overdue has no fixed action in the spec copy, so it stays a fact inside D6 (and in the glance row), not its own action.
- Contract has no `disabled` skip reason: a preview answered `skipped: disabled` returns `reason: null`.
- AI summary language = majority of recipients' locale (shadow only this wave).
- New template keys (product-designer review): `subject.first`, `week`, `glance.*`, `glance.row(First)`, `section.actions`, `section.win`, `D5 action.list/price`, `D6 action.ship/reprints`, `D8 win.bestNet/onTime`, `market.source`, `costLine.*`, `source.*`, `footer.unsubscribe`, `footer.address` (the last two are unused in real mail; T-19-4's footer is used).
