## Round 2
Author: web-engineer on Claude Opus 5.5 (session model: Sonnet 5)

- Fixed per tech lead's round 2 ruling: added `extractTimelineReason(message, from, to)`
  (invai-web/src/features/orders/timeline-reason.ts), a pure helper that strips the backend's
  exact `<from ?? "new"> → <to>` prefix and its ` (`…`)` wrapper from `e.message` to recover
  the raw, untranslated reason. Returns `{type:"reason"}` when matched with a reason,
  `{type:"none"}` when matched with none, `{type:"raw", text: message}` on an unexpected
  format (ruling: show the whole message under the badges in that case).
- `order-detail.tsx` timeline (`Timeline`, ~line 434) now renders the translated badges, then
  the extracted reason text (no translation) or the raw fallback message, right after them.
  No reason parsing for other timeline kinds; AC3 untouched.
- No i18n changes: the reason/fallback text is shown as the backend wrote it, not translated.
  `scripts/i18n-es-missing.json` still absent.
- Unit tests: `timeline-reason.test.ts`, 3 cases (prefix match with reason incl. nested
  parens, prefix match with no reason, unexpected-format fallback incl. mismatched from/to).
