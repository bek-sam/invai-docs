## Decisions
- Three-way intent recorded as keep/drop: InvAI-generated production records are "keep (not Amazon data)". Item titles and refund notes kept and flagged OI-19 (card: keep when unsure).
- `channel_line_id` kept: Amazon order-item code reconciles settlement lines and late refunds.
- `channel_updated_at` kept: nulling it would let a stale payload overwrite a final order.
