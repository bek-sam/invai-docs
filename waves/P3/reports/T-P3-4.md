## Decisions
- **Root cause, not mine to fix:** `finance/service.ts` (`recomputeProfit`, line ~605-633,
  comment "reprints earn nothing") treats `isReprint` as if a reprint item is always an *extra*
  duplicate row that doesn't double-earn revenue. But `production/floor.ts` `openReprint`
  (line ~640) flips `isReprint` on the *same* `order_items` row — it never adds a sibling item.
  So when every item on an order needed a reprint (production failure, not a cancellation), the
  order's revenue and non-reprint unit count both go to 0 in `profit_lines`, and
  `analytics.losingOrders` (my owned function) only sums/counts what's already there. This is
  consistent with every other table that reads `profit_lines` (`getProfit`, `unitEconomics` both
  go through the same `computeNet`/`isUnit` in `analytics/shared.ts`), so `losingOrders` is not
  the odd one out — fixing revenue allocation for reprinted items is a `finance/service.ts`
  change (owner: backend-engineer, finance module), out of my owned paths (`finance-service.ts`
  `losingOrders` only). Flagging to the tech lead as a follow-up card.
- **Likely seed-realism issue, not just a logic one:** of 6,391 seeded order items, only 57 are
  `isReprint`, and those 57 land in exactly 44 orders — every one of those 44 orders is **100%**
  reprinted (no order has a mix of reprint + non-reprint items). Real QC failures hit individual
  items independently (`reprint_cost.md`: ~3% of items pressed), so a realistic seed would mostly
  produce *partial* reprints scattered across many multi-item orders, with full-order reprints
  rare. Proposed seed change (owner: backend-foundation, the seed script) so `losingOrders`'
  worst-5 list doesn't read as "broken data" to a demo viewer.
- No change to `losingOrders` itself. I'd added a pinning test for this behavior, but per the
  tech lead's decision (see "Tech lead decision" below) it's dropped in favor of tracking the
  real fix as backlog B-242 next wave; see the "Correction" note under Built for how that landed.
