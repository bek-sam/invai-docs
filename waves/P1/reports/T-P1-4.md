## Decisions
- `imaging.preview`'s mock is inline fallback logic in the client (not a separate mock provider
  file), since the grant was the `preview` method "and its mock" inside `client.ts` only. It writes
  a real, valid 64x64 gray PNG built by hand with `node:zlib` (no image lib on the backend side).
- Seed order-item thumbnails: filled only for non-personalized mapped items (cheap, no extra
  imaging calls, from the already-known design preview). Skipped generating previews of
  personalized rendered artwork in the seed — not required by AC4, and would add an imaging call
  per personalized item; left as a follow-up if the team wants order-item thumbnails to look real
  for personalized designs too.

  event — outside this card's grant (`orders/jobs.ts` isn't owned or granted). Flagging to the tech
  lead as a small follow-up card for whoever owns `orders`.

  follow-up, not mine to fix).
