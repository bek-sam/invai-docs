## Decisions
- **AC4 on `/nest`, not `/compose`:** `/compose` only sees one physical sheet; `/nest` already returns the full sheet set, so the length-weighted aggregate lives there as an additive field. Flagging the card-wording mismatch for the tech lead/architect.
- **ICC gating:** unconditional `icc_transform(embedded=True)` cost ~2-20ms/design with no profile (~30% regression, over the 25% budget); gated on `icc-profile-data` presence, back to ~0.03ms/call.
- Mirror flips only the design layer so the label stays readable and the QR stays scannable on a mirrored sheet.

## Performance (imaging-change-with-budget)
Realistic 93-design mix, 3 sheets: 40 designs/238.9in PNG+preview 397 MB peak RSS (baseline 440 MB); 15 designs/56.5in PNG+PDF+preview 477 MB (baseline 495 MB) — no regression either way. `/preview` on one 1200x900 design: ~27ms. `to_srgba()` microbenchmark ~0.03ms/call post-gating vs ~2-20ms/call unconditional. Numbers in `invai-imaging/README.md`.
