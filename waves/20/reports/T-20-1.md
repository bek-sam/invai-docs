## Decisions
- Under-way is signalled by `peakMonth` present and `actByDate` absent (the spec's "carries no act-by date"); no contract change.
- Past-peak read filter keeps `ids` lookups so stored assistant cards can still vote.
- Spanish points use locale `es` (decimal comma, as the approved "+6,9 pts"), while other es numbers stay `es-US` (wave 19 AC13 tests pin "$1,234.56" and "(+23.5%)"). So one line reads "Margen: 31.4% (+3,3 pts ...)". Flag for PM/T-20-2 if they want one convention.
- Short source date uses `es-MX` ("27 sep"; `es-US` writes "sept").
- Pricing mock `asOf` left as "now" (a daily snapshot, never future); the grant is about the demand series.
