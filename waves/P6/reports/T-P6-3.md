## Decisions
- `unauthorized` events are not forwarded to `opts.onMessage` (treated as transport control, consistent with how `ready`/`ping`/`shutdown` are effectively ignored by the only consumer, `useRealtime`'s `QUEUE_EVENTS` filter) — kept the parser callback the single place that branches on event name, rather than pushing filtering into the consumer.
- Fixed the 401-at-connect retry bug (AC2's "also stops retries") in the same `if (res.status === 401)` block rather than adding a second `stopped` check after the try/catch, since the fetch loop never starts for a 401 anyway.
