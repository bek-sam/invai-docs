# Competitive landscape: software for in-house DTF/DTG/heat-press apparel shops that sell on marketplaces (as of Sep 2026)

I ran about 50 web searches and fetched about 15 pages. **Limits you should know about:**
- Reddit, Facebook groups and G2 pages mostly did not show up in search or could not be fetched. T-shirtforums redirected to a paywall. So the complaints below come mainly from Capterra, Software Advice, Shopify App Store and review blogs.
- Prices come from vendor pages or third-party review sites and can change.
- Three names on your list are not what the brief assumed:
  - **Printfuse** is an Indian POD dropshipper founded in 2024, not shop-management software ([printfuse.in](https://printfuse.in/)).
  - **"Pod.ai"** could not be identified as a product.
  - **Kothari** is a DTF RIP (the software that drives the printer), not a gang-sheet SaaS.

## 2. The closest direct competitors (found during research, not on your list)

- **Pythias Technologies Fulfillment Cloud**
  - Pitches itself as the "production-floor OS" for garment print shops.
  - Four stations: pull orders into one queue, produce (DTG/DTF/screen/embroidery, with a Brother GTX printer integration), ship with live rates and labels, sync tracking back.
  - Channels: Shopify, TikTok Shop, Walmart, Etsy, Amazon, eBay, Kohl's, Target Plus.
  - Also claims gang-sheet management, blank inventory with reorder alerts, scan-to-complete label printing and badge login.
  - Pricing: Starter $199 (500 orders, 2 integrations), Professional $599, Business $1,499, Scale $3,000/mo. Onboarding takes 1–2 weeks.
  - Origin: built as the internal system for TShirtPalace ($1M to $10M a year). Still collecting a "founding cohort" of 100 shops, so it is early-stage.
  - **This is the most direct threat.** Its weak points are price for small shops (only 2 channels at $199) and no visible listing, SEO or design AI.
  - Sources: https://pythiastechnologies.com/, https://pythiastechnologies.com/pricing, https://pythiastechnologies.com/compare/best-dtf-fulfillment-software
- **MyDesigns.io**
  - Tools for POD sellers: AI design, mockups, bulk publishing of up to 120 listings, and AI listing SEO.
  - Publishes to Etsy, Shopify, WooCommerce and TikTok Shop; Amazon connection also mentioned.
  - Pricing: Free, Starter $19.99, Pro $39.99, Pro Plus $79.99/mo.
  - It has a **"Self Fulfillment"** mode, but it only turns off routing to a POD provider. You approve and fulfill each order by hand and download the print file. There is no blank inventory, gang-sheet building, production queue or label workflow.
  - **This is the most direct threat on the selling side.**
  - Sources: https://mydesigns.io/pricing, https://mydesigns.io/blog/introducing-mydesigns-new-self-fulfillment-feature/
- **Craftybase**
  - Maker inventory, raw materials and cost-of-goods (COGS) accounting, mainly for Etsy.
  - Studio plan $49/mo for up to 250 order lines.
  - Not specific to printing.
  - Source: https://craftybase.com/etsy-inventory-software
- **PrintShopCRM**
  - Flat-price CRM positioned against Printavo, with an embeddable DTF gang-sheet builder.
  - Source: https://printshopcrm.com/blog/best-printavo-alternative-2026/

## 3. POD networks: the model to copy, and the alternative your customers are choosing against

- **Printify**
  - Free plan. Premium went from $29 to **$39/mo on Feb 17, 2026** ($299/yr); it gives up to 20% off products.
  - Has an AI image generator (OpenAI-powered, 15 per day) and AI mockups (Premium, 10 per day).
  - Supports TikTok Shop (US only). Walmart only via Order Desk.
  - Complaint: inconsistent quality across its print providers.
  - Sources: https://mydesigns.io/blog/printify-pricing-changes-2026/, https://printify.com/blog/ai-image-generator/, https://help.printify.com/hc/en-us/articles/18312840591249
- **Printful**
  - No monthly fee; pays per order; higher unit costs.
  - Merged with Printify into **FYUL** (announced Nov 2024). Both still run as separate brands as of mid-2026. Sellers worry about less competition on price.
  - Sources: https://traksource.com/printify-review/, https://www.gelato.com/blog/printful-and-printify-merger
- **CustomCat:** Lite plan free; PRO $25–30/mo; US orders ship in 1–3 days. **Gelato, Teelaunch, Merchize:** pay per order. **Gooten:** no subscription, base costs 10–20% below Printful, uneven quality because it routes orders to partners, and now connected to KornitX.
  - Sources: https://www.gelato.com/blog/best-print-on-demand-companies, https://merchtitans.com/blog/gooten-print-on-demand-review
- **Important:** Printful and Printify do not offer automated DTF the way they offer DTG, "so most DTF-based POD sellers run their own fulfillment." That is structural demand for in-house DTF software.
  - Source: https://pythiastechnologies.com/compare/best-dtf-fulfillment-software
- A shop can also join Printify as a white-label print provider. Onboarding takes weeks to months. That lets in-house shops pick up overflow orders, a possible network play.
  - Source: https://merch.printify.com/become-a-print-provider/

## 6. Gang-sheet and DTF tools

- **Build A Gang Sheet (Drip Apps):** revenue share; used by 4,600 shops. Shopify and WordPress only. Some reviews allege lock-outs while still being billed.
- **Antigro Gang Sheet Builder:** 3–8% of order value; auto-builder is well reviewed.
- **DTF Gang Sheet App:** from $40/mo or $0.15/sheet; AI auto-nest; exports ready for RIPs including Kothari.
- **Kixxl:** free up to 100 sq ft; Shopify and WooCommerce.
- **CADlink Digital Factory** (now Fiery): the RIP itself. Large Format Production edition about $850. Has auto-nesting, hot folders, barcodes and multi-printer control.

**Takeaway:** all of these builders face **B2B customers who buy transfers**. None of them turns a shop's own Etsy or Amazon orders into nested sheets automatically.

## 8. Gap analysis: the local shop that owns its printer and blanks and sells on marketplaces

**Today's typical tool stack** for a shop of 1–15 people:
- Printify or MyDesigns, or manual work, for listings
- eRank or Alura for SEO
- Placeit for mockups
- ShipStation or Veeqo for labels
- A spreadsheet for blanks
- Photoshop or a RIP to lay out gang sheets by hand
- Sometimes Printavo, but that is built for B2B work

That is roughly $150–400/mo across 5–8 tools, and nothing connects the listing to the production floor.

**Nobody does these well:**
1. **Marketplace orders become production work automatically.** Decorator software (Printavo, DecoNetwork, YoPrint, shopVOX, Teesom) has no native Etsy, Amazon, TikTok or Walmart. Order and shipping tools (ShipStation, Veeqo, Sellbrite) have no idea what a print job is. Only Pythias spans both, starting at $199 with 2 channels.
2. **Blank inventory as a shared material list.** Each listing variant should map to a blank (brand, style, color, size) plus a print file and placement. Stock should be decremented from the shared pool, reorder alerts should fire, and purchase orders should go to S&S, SanMar or Alphabroder. Today this is spreadsheets, Craftybase (not print-aware), YoPrint Pro (no marketplaces) or Fulfill Engine ($500/mo).
3. **Automatic gang sheets from today's orders.** Batch open orders by due date and ship-by SLA, auto-nest the DTF print files, and export to a RIP hot folder (CADlink or Kothari) with barcodes. Existing builders only serve transfer customers.
4. **Scan-to-ship tied to marketplace deadlines.** Scan the pressed garment, print a cheap USPS or UPS label, and push tracking to Etsy, Amazon or TikTok before their late-shipment penalties. Pythias and Fulfill Engine have pieces of this; smaller tools do not.
5. **Profit per order with every cost included.** Blank cost, film, ink and powder per square inch, marketplace fees (Etsy, Amazon, TikTok, Walmart), shipping and ad spend. Nobody combines these.
6. **One place to list everywhere with AI, for a shop that makes its own goods.** MyDesigns and Vela cover part of it (Vela has no Amazon, TikTok or Walmart). Nobody pairs listing with an in-house catalog of what the shop can actually print, a trademark check before publishing, and Etsy "made in-house" disclosure compliance.
7. **Pricing that fits small shops.** Incumbents are per-seat, rising in price, or enterprise-only (Cin7, Linnworks, Rithum, GoFlow at $499+). The recurring complaints are price increases, forced payment processors and slow onboarding.

**Where an integrated platform could win:**
- A single chain: listing to order to blank reservation to auto gang sheet to RIP hot folder to press queue to scan-to-label to tracking sync.
- Priced roughly $49–149/mo with no per-order fees, undercutting Pythias ($199+) and Fulfill Engine ($500), and replacing the ShipStation price hike.
- AI in places competitors do not have it:
  - Listing and SEO generation per marketplace
  - AI mockups from the shop's actual blanks
  - Trademark and IP risk screening before publishing
  - Demand forecasting to drive blank reorders
  - Auto-nesting
  - A chat assistant that answers questions like "what's my margin on TikTok this week"
- Optional overflow routing to Printify or CustomCat when capacity is full, a hybrid that neither pure POD nor pure shop software offers.
- **Main risks:** Pythias is moving into exactly this segment; MyDesigns could add production features; Inktavo has a large distribution base.
