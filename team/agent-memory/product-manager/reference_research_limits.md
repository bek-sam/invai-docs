Reddit (fetch and search), T-Shirt Forums (tollbit redirect), Etsy Community post bodies and Facebook groups are unreachable to agents (confirmed in research 03 and 16). Seller voice has to come from Amazon Seller Forums, Shopify Community, Trustpilot, Shopify App Store and Capterra reviews, trade press (Impressions, Apparelist). Seller-center fee pages need a login, so fee changes are often [3P] only.
**How to apply:** say so in the limits section; ask the owner for pilot interviews to fill the gap. Related: [[growth-research-16]].
