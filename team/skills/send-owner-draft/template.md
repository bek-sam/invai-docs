# Owner draft entry

Append to the end of `invai-docs/owner-inbox.md`. Keep the heading on one line.

````
## OI-<n>: DRAFT <kind> to <recipient> — <subject>   status: open
- From: <role>, <YYYY-MM-DD>. Deadline: <YYYY-MM-DD and why (clock or marketplace date)>. Default if no answer: not sent
- Kind: email / reply / portal form / submission / questionnaire / web page / post / listing / legal draft
- To: <organisation and role, e.g. "Etsy developer support", "pilot shop Desert Bloom, owner">. Address or URL: <where the owner sends it, or "owner knows">
- Send when: <date, or trigger such as "after OI-12 is answered">
- Attachments: <paths in invai-docs/…, or none>
- Reviewed by: <role(s) and date>, compliance-officer: <yes / not needed because …>
- Claims and sources: <path to the claims list or packet, or "none">
- Needs owner input: <lines marked [owner to confirm], or none>
- Draft:
  ```
  Subject: <subject, if email>

  <full text exactly as it should be sent>
  ```
- Answer:
````

Example of a good default: "Default if no answer: not sent; the Shopify App Store submission slips by one week."
