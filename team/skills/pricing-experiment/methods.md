# Pricing methods, by sample size

| Shops available | Method | What it tells you | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2–10 (pilots) | **Current-stack interview.** Owner asks what they pay today for ShipStation or Pirate Ship, the gang-sheet service, listing and SEO tools, mockups, spreadsheets and staff hours (concept "Questions for pilot shops", #9 and #11). | The ceiling they compare us against | Shops forget small subscriptions; ask for the invoices |
| 2–10 | **Real offer at pilot end.** One plan, one price, one label fee, with a deadline. | Whether they pay. The only strong signal at this size | One offer per shop; write down objections word for word |
| 5–30 | **Van Westendorp** (4 questions: too cheap, a bargain, getting expensive, too expensive) | An acceptable range, as input | Stated answers overestimate willingness to pay; small samples are fine only as input to further work |
| 5–30 | **Gabor-Granger** (would you buy at $X? then a higher or lower $X) | A rough demand curve | Same bias; anchor on the full monthly bill |
| 100+ trials (after launch) | **Pricing-page or checkout test** (growth-marketer) | Conversion by price | Needs a sample-size check from the data-analyst; never show two prices to the same shop |
| Any | **Plan-fit analysis** (data-analyst): usage against tier caps | Whether caps sit in the right place | Seasonal: Q4 and TikTok spikes break monthly caps |

## Interview script skeleton (draft for the owner; `send-owner-draft`)
1. "Walk me through what you pay each month to run orders, labels, gang sheets and listings."
2. "Which of those would you drop if InvAI did it?"
3. "Here's the full monthly bill for your volume: plan $X plus about $Y in label fees. How does that compare?"
4. The Van Westendorp or Gabor-Granger questions, if the plan uses them.
5. "What would stop you from signing up at this price?"

Sources: [Adience on B2B pricing research](https://www.adience.com/blog/how-to/how-to-conduct-b2b-pricing-research-tools-and-advice-from-adience/), [Irrational Labs on willingness to pay](https://irrationallabs.com/blog/ultimate-guide-willingness-to-pay/), [Verint on Van Westendorp](https://www.verint.com/blog/the-van-westendorp-price-sensitivity-meter/) (checked 2026-09-24).
