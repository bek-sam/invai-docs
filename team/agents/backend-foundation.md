## How the foundation works (as built)
- **DB access:**
  - `db` connects as the app role `invai_app` (RLS applies). `withTenant(companyId, fn)` sets `app.company_id` transaction-locally, and `withVendor(vendorOrgId, fn)` does the same for vendor policies.
  - `withSystem(fn, companyId?)` uses the owner connection and is only for the relay, cross-tenant jobs and the seed.
  - `afterCommit(tx, fn)` runs a side effect after the transaction commits.
- **Schema:**
  - 61+ tables in `src/db/schema/<module>.ts`, snake_case, each with `company_id`, `tenantPolicy()` and `.enableRLS()`.
  - Vendor read/update policies go through `vendor_access`, granted by the sheet's own company, and a trigger blocks `company_id` changes.
  - Better Auth tables have no RLS by design.
- **Migrations:**
  - Generated by drizzle-kit into `drizzle/`, plus custom SQL: grants, trigram GIN indexes, the append-only trigger on `inventory_movements`, and REVOKE update/delete on audit and transition tables.
  - `pnpm db:migrate` runs as the owner.
- **Events:** `emit(tx, companyId, name, payload)` writes the outbox in the same transaction. The relay (`FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`) fans out to jobs registered with `defineJob` plus `onEvent`. Job ids go through `safeJobId`, because BullMQ forbids `:`.
- **Realtime:** `publish(companyId, name, payload)` sends the contract's `RealtimeEnvelope` to a capped Redis Stream and pub/sub. SSE `/events` accepts a cookie, `Bearer`, or `?token=` (floor), and replays from `Last-Event-ID`.
- **Auth:**
  - Better Auth with email + password and the organization plugin: companies are organizations with `type` shop or vendor.
  - Org mutation endpoints are disabled through `disabledPaths`; team changes go through `team.*` with owner-protection rules.
  - Rate limits: sign-in 20/min/IP.
- **Floor auth:**
  - Station token `st1.<companyId>.<random>` (sha256 stored, sent as `Authorization: Station …`).
  - PIN HMAC per company, locked for 15 minutes after 10 failures per station.
  - Signed 12h floor session `fs1.…` sent as a Bearer; revocation and role changes take effect immediately.
- **Router and guard:**
  - `api/orpc.ts` provides `os`, `pub` and `authed` (the guard reads the contract's `meta.auth` and `meta.permission` and checks `ROLE_PERMISSIONS`).
  - `api/router.ts` composes the module routers, and `stubRouter()` returns NOT_IMPLEMENTED for missing procedures.
- **State machine:** `transitionItem(tx, id, to, {actor, stationKind?, reason?, data?})` validates against `ITEM_TRANSITIONS` and writes the transition, audit, outbox and realtime (after commit). `recomputeOrderStatus` uses `deriveOrderStatus`.
- **Crypto:** AES-256-GCM `encryptedText()` columns with a key id prefix (`FIELD_ENCRYPTION_KEY` = `k1:<base64 32B>`; KMS is planned).
- **Files:** keys are `{companyId}/{kind}/...`. `presignPut` binds content type and size. `isCompanyKey` and `isSafeKey` guard access.

## Rules
1. Any new tenant table: `company_id`, a tenant policy and RLS, indexes on (company_id, the usual filter). The RLS coverage test must stay green.
2. Never use `withSystem` in a request path without a written reason in code.
3. A new cross-cutting helper goes in `src/lib`, with a test and a line in `src/modules/README.md`.
4. **The seed must stay realistic and fast** (about 25 s). It uses the real services and state machine where practical, a realistic size mix (adult 10.5×12, youth 8.5×9.5, chest 3.75, sleeve 3×10, back 12×14), and enough stock that nothing goes negative, and it renders real art through imaging.
5. Keep `.env.example` in sync with `src/env.ts`.
